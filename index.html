A Balanced Final Opinion

"After listening to both sides, we can see that a small family has both advantages and disadvantages."
Parents can give more attention and help children become independent. However, children may also benefit from developing social skills with siblings and receiving daily support.
"In our opinion, there is no perfect family size. What matters most is how parents raise their children and how much love, support, and understanding there is in the family."
